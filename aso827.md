## Executive directive

Keep the `Bond` brand and the current App Store title. The title owns the app's strongest real US search cluster: #2 for `love language reminder`, #2 for `love language reminders`, and #7 for `love language`. Do not reset that cluster.

Replace the subtitle. `Love Language Reminder App` repeats almost every meaningful word in `Bond: Love Language Reminders`, spending 26 visible characters to add only the generic word `App`. Use the subtitle to expose the product's real second and third pillars: private couples check-ins and shared milestones.

Rebuild the hidden keyword field around combinations the product can satisfy. Remove broad, unranked `notes` and the redundant `couple` token once `Couples` is in the subtitle. Add literal long-distance and gift intent while preserving partner, marriage, anniversary, widget, questions, and relationship combinations.

Rebuild the screenshot package through the shared screenshot system. The live iPhone set has only five frames and uses an obsolete headline-plus-subline system. Its milestone and widget data is now stale, and there is no canonical capture report. Keep the cream visual identity and large phone, but use one period-free benefit header, an eight-frame iPhone story, and a separate narrowly truthful Watch set.

## Positioning and competitor gap

Recommended position:

> Thoughtful reminders, private daily check-ins, and milestones for one relationship

Bond should not compete as an all-purpose couples social network or relationship tracker. Its differentiated job is turning partner knowledge into timely action.

The current result set divides into three crowded positions: relationship curriculum, private couples social space, and shared planning. Bond's defensible fourth position is a thoughtful-action system. Its search and screenshot story should make that difference visible in this order:

1. Remember a partner-specific action at the useful moment.
2. Start from love-language-aware ideas instead of a blank reminder.
3. Reinforce the relationship with one private daily question and meaningful dates.

Do not borrow the competitors' strongest but unsupported claims. `Scientifically proven`, `better relationship`, `reduce stress`, `secure`, and official Love Language branding require evidence or rights Bond does not currently have.

## Product truth and claim boundaries

Supported by the current source and metadata:

- Personalized reminders mapped to acts of service, words of affirmation, quality time, gifts, and touch
- Reminder templates for date nights, daily appreciation, long distance, new parents, and other situations
- One paired partner through an invite flow
- Daily check-in questions with both answers revealed under the product's rules
- Shared milestones and anniversary countdowns
- Home Screen widgets
- Apple Watch voice reminder entry
- Sign in with Apple
- Optional Bond+ subscription and trial
- No ads and no sale of user data, subject to the exact privacy policy

Avoid or qualify:

- `Private` must match the Supabase storage, access controls, and privacy policy. Do not imply local-only storage.
- Do not describe pairing as anonymous or end-to-end encrypted without proof.
- Do not claim reminders make a partner happier or improve relationship health.
- Do not call check-ins therapy, counseling, attachment work, or a diagnostic assessment.
- Do not imply that all features are free when templates, check-ins, stats, widgets, or other surfaces may be gated.
- Do not claim real-time partner presence unless the current sync behavior proves it.
- Do not use absolute copy such as `Never forget again` or `Save your relationship`.

Retain Lifestyle as primary. Health & Fitness is acceptable as secondary only with non-clinical wellbeing framing. If future copy becomes therapy-adjacent, remove or review it before release.
