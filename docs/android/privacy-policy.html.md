## Little Gestures Android Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026

## Your identity and couple

Little Gestures, also known as Bond, creates an anonymous account automatically. Android does not require an email address, password or social sign-in. Supabase stores your anonymous account identifier, couple membership, display preferences, reminders and their completion or reaction events, check-in responses and milestones. This enables solo use and the couple features you choose to use. Information you share in a couple is available to your partner.

## Reminders and device permissions

Android schedules local notifications with your permission. Couple changes refresh when you return or refresh the screen. Android does not request or track your current device location. An existing arrival reminder created on iPhone can contain saved place details or coordinates; Android preserves that reminder information, and its arrival alerts fire on iPhone. No contacts, microphone or photo permission is requested.

## Purchases and app interactions

Google Play processes purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, transaction and entitlement information, limited technical context, and counts or dates of app opens and purchase-screen interactions. These help deliver purchases, restore access and understand purchase flows. We do not send reminder text, check-in answers or saved places to RevenueCat. We do not use an advertising SDK or sell your information. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Delete your account and data

In the Android app, open Settings > Account > Delete account, then confirm Delete everything. This removes your anonymous identity and the content you created. A former partner keeps their own reminders, shared milestones and account, and becomes unpaired. Leaving a couple alone does not delete your account. Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription or erase purchase records retained for billing, legal obligations or fraud prevention. Manage subscriptions in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

You can also request help with deletion at [jackwallner+b@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner+b@gmail.com?subject=Little%20Gestures%20Android%20data%20deletion). Say that your request concerns Little Gestures on Android. Because your identity is anonymous, we may need information from the app to locate it. Do not email passwords or payment details.

## Retention, security and support

Your account and couple records remain until deleted through the app or a valid deletion request. Network connections to the backend and billing services use HTTPS. Support messages contain what you choose to send. This adult relationship routine app does not knowingly collect children's information. We will update this page if practices change.
