Little Gestures Android Terms

Updated October 18, 2026

Using the app

Little Gestures is a reminder and relationship routine tool. It does not provide counseling, diagnosis or treatment. Use it solo or pair with a partner who chooses to join. Do not enter information you do not have permission to share. A paired partner can see the couple information shared by the app.

Android behavior

Notifications are local and can be delayed by Android settings. Couple information refreshes on return or refresh. New arrival reminders, Apple sign-in, Apple Watch and iOS widgets are not included. Existing iPhone arrival reminders explain that arrival alerts fire on iPhone. Removing the app or its data can remove access to an anonymous session; delete the account in Settings first if you want its backend records removed.

Optional Little Gestures+

Google Play displays the applicable plan price, billing period and any eligible trial before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. If an eligible trial is offered and not cancelled before it ends, billing begins at the price shown by Google Play. Manage or cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time unlock. Restore using the same Google account. Account deletion and leaving a couple do not cancel subscriptions. Refund requests follow Google Play's rules.
