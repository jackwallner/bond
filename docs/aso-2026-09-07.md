# September 7, 2026 App Store positioning update

Target: Bond 1.0.6 (70), all 50 App Store Connect text locales, release automatically after approval.

## Decision

Keep the indexed love-language/reminder title. Use the subtitle for couples, dates and anniversaries, with relationship-intent keywords instead of repeated title words.

## Evidence

ASC browser totals, June 9-September 6: 43 first-time downloads and 7,122 impressions. These are separate measures, not Apple's conversion-rate denominator.

July 27 title changes were followed by 7 downloads / 1,436 impressions over July 28-August 10, versus 7 / 914 over July 13-26. More visibility did not produce more downloads in these tiny windows. US Astro ranks love language reminder #2; broaden supporting intent without discarding that anchor.

The signed-in Apple Ads account has no campaign for this app. All 11 account campaigns were paused or on hold in the inspected view. No paid campaign or spend was started. Detailed ASC API reports are incomplete or privacy-suppressed and were not treated as zero-download evidence. Earlier om97/lm97 analyses were hypotheses, checked against current source data.

## Verification

Existing processed TestFlight build 70 is attached to the new review draft. The app test suite passed on a leased headless simulator; no production RevenueCat key was configured. No app source changes were needed for this positioning update. Strict upload validates exact character limits, all 50 locale sets, privacy and EULA links, then compares every uploaded field against ASC. Review notes describe current free, paid and hardware flows. Current subscription prices and eligible seven-day trials were verified; no pricing changes were made.

Approved screenshots are preserved. Text localization does not change the app's interface language or imply localized editorial content.

Compare 14- and 28-day impressions, first-time downloads, product-page conversion, trial starts per new download and paid conversion.
