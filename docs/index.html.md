## Frequently asked questions

## Is there an app that reminds you of your partner's love language?

Yes. Little Gestures lets you set reminders around the five love languages (words of affirmation, acts of service, gifts, quality time and touch) so a thoughtful gesture happens even in a busy week. It is a free iPhone app with an Apple Watch companion.

## Is Little Gestures free?

Yes, it is free to download. You can build your own love-language reminders and milestones for free, and the optional Bond+ upgrade adds daily check-ins, insights, location reminders and template packs. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

## Does my partner need the app too?

No. You can create and use your own reminders without your partner installing anything. If you want shared milestones and the daily check-in, send your partner an invite link and they tap it to pair.

## What is a good app to remember anniversaries and important dates?

Little Gestures keeps anniversaries, first dates and other milestones on one shared timeline. It sends heads-up notifications before the day and shows countdowns in home screen widgets.

## Does it work for long-distance couples?

Yes. Reminders, milestones and the daily check-in do not depend on being in the same place, and Bond+ includes curated templates for long distance and date nights.

## Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. You can add a reminder by voice from your Apple Watch and it is scheduled right away. Widgets also show your next reminder and milestone countdown on your iPhone.

## Do I need an account?

You sign in with Apple, or start anonymously and upgrade to a full account later. The app has no ads and does not sell your data.
