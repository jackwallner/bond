Bond Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Bond is a couples app for reminders, shared milestones, daily check-ins, and relationship insights. It requires an account so partners can share selected content.

Information we collect

  • Sign in with Apple account identifiers and the profile information you choose to provide.
  • Pairing, reminders, milestones, check-ins, voice or text content, and settings you create in the app.
  • Location information when you enable location-based reminders or relationship features. Bond uses it for the enabled feature and does not continuously track your location.
  • Purchase and entitlement information needed to provide Bond+.

How we use and share it

Supabase hosts account and shared app data so your paired devices can sync. Apple provides authentication and payments. RevenueCat verifies Bond+ purchases and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical context. These providers process data to provide their services, not for our advertising. We do not sell personal information and do not use third-party behavioral advertising.

Notifications and permissions

Reminders and milestone alerts are scheduled locally when possible. You can disable notifications and revoke location access in iOS Settings. Sign in with Apple never gives Bond your Apple password.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and shared content while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Use the in-app account controls or email us to request deletion. We will delete the profile, shared content, pairing records, and other account data we control within 30 days of a verified request, except for records we must retain. Apple and RevenueCat keep transaction records under their policies.

Children

Bond is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
