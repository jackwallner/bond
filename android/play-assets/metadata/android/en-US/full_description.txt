Turn what matters to your partner into small actions you remember. Little Gestures helps you keep thoughtful reminders, shared milestones and a daily rhythm of care.

START SOLO OR PAIR UP
Use Little Gestures on your own, or invite your partner with a pairing code. An anonymous identity is created automatically, with no email, password or social sign-in. Your reminders and couple information are stored securely in the shared backend so paired partners can use them together.

REMEMBER THE SMALL THINGS
Record preferences, make reminders and mark gestures done. Choose local reminder times, keep milestones in view and return to what your partner appreciates. Android notifications can be delayed by battery settings.

OPTIONAL LITTLE GESTURES+
Explore personalized ideas, daily check-ins, love-language insights and surprise reminders. The app shows the applicable plans and any eligible trial before purchase through Google Play. Restore access with the same Google account.

MADE FOR ANDROID
Works solo and with a partner. Couple information refreshes when you return or refresh the screen. Arrival alerts, Apple Watch, iOS widgets and Apple sign-in are not included. Existing arrival reminders created on iPhone explain that those alerts fire on iPhone.

YOU CONTROL YOUR ACCOUNT
Leave a couple or delete your account in Settings. Account deletion removes your identity and the content you created; your former partner keeps their own reminders, shared milestones and account. A Google Play subscription is managed separately in Google Play.

Little Gestures is a reminder and relationship routine tool. It does not provide counseling, diagnosis or treatment.
