Relationship reminders for couples who want to show up in small ways. Little Gestures turns what matters to your partner into thoughtful actions you remember, with shared milestones and a daily rhythm of care.

START SOLO OR PAIR UP
Use Little Gestures on your own, or invite your partner with a pairing code. An anonymous identity is created automatically, with no email, password or social sign-in. Your reminders and couple information are stored securely in the shared backend so paired partners can use them together.

REMEMBER THE SMALL THINGS
Record what your partner appreciates, make reminders for anniversaries, birthdays and date nights, and mark gestures done. Choose local reminder times, keep milestones in view and return to what your partner loves. Android notifications can be delayed by battery settings.

OPTIONAL LITTLE GESTURES+
Explore personalised ideas, daily check-ins, love-language insights and surprise reminders. The app shows the applicable plans and any eligible trial before purchase through Google Play. Restore access with the same Google account.

MADE FOR ANDROID
Works solo and with a partner. Couple information refreshes when you return or refresh the screen. Arrival alerts, a watch app and home-screen widgets are not included on Android.

YOU CONTROL YOUR ACCOUNT
Leave a couple or delete your account in Settings. Account deletion removes your identity and the content you created; your former partner keeps their own reminders, shared milestones and account. A Google Play subscription is managed separately in Google Play.

Little Gestures is a reminder and relationship routine tool. It does not provide counselling, diagnosis or treatment.
