## What survived, ranked by users reached times cost

1. **B1** Unpair keeps delivering, and keeps the ex able to send new text. Proven in SQL. Safety issue, every unpaired couple that had partner reminders.
2. **N1 (missed)** The daily check-in question changes at 00:00 UTC, not local midnight: 4 PM in Los Angeles, 7 PM in New York, 9 AM in Tokyo (`DailyCheckInService.swift:37-43`). A partner who answers before that hour and one who answers after are on different questions, so neither sees the reveal for that day, and there is no history screen to find it later. Same-zone couples in the Americas, every day, on the headline Bond+ feature. Proven on iOS 27.2 (run 2 above) and on Linux swift-foundation. Settle the fix by deriving the day from the local date (`dateComponents([.year, .month, .day])`) or a couple-agreed zone, and decide which; that is the product call B7 was reaching for.
3. **B4** Insights leak the partner's private reminder titles (every paired Bond+ couple).
4. **B9** Recurring "Starting" ignored on device (free tier, deterministic).
5. **B3** Daily template packs stack at 9:00 every day (Bond+ importers of 3 of 6 packs).
6. **B5** Banner tap while editing overwrites another reminder (rare, destructive).
7. **B2** Handled one-shots re-armed after a half-failed launch.
8. **B6** Daily random stops after 5 idle days.
9. **B13**, 10. **B14**, 11. **B12**, 12. **B8**, 13. **B11**, 14. **B10**, 15. **B15**, 16. **B16**.

Ranking changes against the audit: B3 and B9 move up (deterministic, recurring, free tier for B9) and B2 moves down (needs a partial failure, self reminders only, heals on the next foreground). B13 and B14 are rarer than their table position implied.

## Product calls

PC-1 to PC-9 hold as product calls; each is either documented (`ReminderCompletion.swift:3-7` for PC-1, `NotificationScheduler.swift:24-26` for PC-2, the surprise comment at `ReminderEditorView.swift:539-541` for PC-4) or low impact. PC-1 still deserves a decision: rescheduling a handled one-shot from the Handled list silently never fires, locally (`NotificationScheduler.swift:51`) and on the server (`reminder_done_now`, `0014:155-160`).

**PC-10 is wrong.** It says reminders written for the ex "stay on the author's list with nobody to receive them". They are received: that is B1, proven above. Fold it into B1.

## Method changes that would have prevented the errors

1. **Run the exact expression on the target OS before building a claim on it.** B7 assumed `ordinality(of:in:for:)` follows the calendar's zone. One probe would have refuted it and pointed at N1 instead.
2. **No test may reach the live backend.** sm4's precondition is the production server returning no profile row, and the first sm5 draft hit it too. Inject the failure, or mark the claim Code path.
3. **Look for headless seams beyond the app's own.** B1 sat in Code path although `scripts/test-db.sh` exists and Postgres runs in Docker in minutes. B3, B9 and B10 were blocked on `UNUserNotificationCenter`, but `nextTriggerDate()` answers the question without permission.
4. **Trace each route through who delivers it.** Partner rows are server-delivered once push is registered, which changes B2's reach and makes B14's chosen route moot.
5. **Check every "always" and every citation.** "Silent-push launches always start with `events == []`" contradicts `BondApp.swift:37-40`; B14 cites a rule the code follows.
6. **Cross-check product calls against the bugs.** PC-10 contradicts B1.
7. **Assert the user-visible symptom, not the fix.** sm5 asserts "no rebuild", the auditor's remedy; sm6 asserts the pure function with two events but not that two events can arise.
